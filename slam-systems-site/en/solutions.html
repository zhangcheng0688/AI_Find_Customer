SLAM.SYSTEMS Solutions

SLAM.SYSTEMSSolutions

SLAM.SYSTEMS brings together more than a quarter of a century of experience in developing, integrating and operating professional communication and media systems, including OOHDT, CORPORATEDIS TV, CORPORATEDIS AUDIO, CORPORATEDIS TXT and digital signage solutions.

This experience comes from connecting different areas of technology: sports and network media playout systems, professional LED solutions, and their integration into real applications and demanding live environments.

Our solutions create reliable system environments that remain stable over the long term while adapting flexibly to new technologies, requirements and future developments.

  • Integration and use of existing infrastructure Connecting existing systems with new technologies and solutions.
  • Custom development Individual adaptations and extensions for specific applications and requirements.
  • Manufacturer visits and test operation Support with evaluation, testing and technical coordination.
  • Installation and commissioning Professional implementation and integration on site.
