The Intelligent Control Deck for Professional Sports Communication

SLAM DECKCOR is the central operating and control environment within the SLAM.SYSTEMSCOR Family. Connecting the operator and the system, SLAM DECKCOR provides an intuitive interface for controlling modern sports and event environments.

From here, media content, LED systems and live processes can be controlled simply and reliably.

Developed for professional sports environments, SLAM DECKCOR combines a clear overview with ease of use. The graphical interface on iPad Pro enables an intuitive workflow and central control of everything from individual LED systems to complex, networked display environments. Even in demanding live scenarios, operation remains clear and straightforward, allowing one person to confidently monitor and control the entire sequence.

The system architecture is built on stability, flexibility and expandability. Its modular design allows SLAM DECKCOR to be adapted to different requirements and expanded at any time.

Frontend

SLAM SCORECOR

Connecting sport data. Connecting visual communication.

As a specialised frontend within the SLAM.SYSTEMSCOR Family, SLAM SCORECOR was developed to control professional scoreboard and live information systems simply, flexibly and reliably. Its clearly structured iPad Pro interface allows referees or operators to manage all relevant content confidently and efficiently during live operation.

SLAM SCORECOR is individually configured for sports, leagues and competitions, adapting to their specific requirements — from rules and data structures to individual layouts and visual presentations. Together with SLAM PLAYCOR, it forms a powerful standalone solution designed for different LED scoreboard formats and customer-specific display requirements.

As requirements grow, the system can be expanded with additional components from the SLAM.SYSTEMSCOR Family. SLAM DECKCOR and SLAM DIRECTORCOR create a central environment for managing match information, sponsor and advertising communication, media content, live TV on the scoreboard and other visual elements.

Integrated broadcast interfaces allow official match times and results to be synchronised with external production systems. SLAM SCORECOR also supports additional data sources, such as Kinexon real-time location systems (RTLS), capturing position and movement data and displaying them as visual information on the LED scoreboard.

SLAM DIRECTORCOR allows multimedia shows to be programmed in advance and executed automatically during live operation. Media sequences, visual effects and external systems such as lighting and sound can be integrated with coordinated timing and controlled as part of the overall live experience.

SLAM SCORECOR combines sports logic, live data, media content and visual communication in an intelligent frontend for modern sports presentation.

Control

SLAM DIRECTORCOR

The Intelligent Control Layer Behind Every Live Experience

SLAM DIRECTORCOR is the central environment for creating, managing and executing professional media layouts and live scenarios. Through layer-based composition of media sequences, visual effects and external systems, SLAM DIRECTORCOR enables precise design of scalable live experiences — from individual display applications to fully integrated multimedia shows in stadiums, event venues and other live environments.

Within the SLAM.SYSTEMSCOR Family, SLAM DIRECTORCOR centrally manages content, including its scaling, positioning and timing for the defined output systems. Content can be displayed automatically on LED systems or other displays at the desired time, size and position.

The system combines creative possibilities with intelligent control. Media and TV content, digital signage applications and external systems such as lighting, sound and third-party peripherals are integrated through defined interfaces and controlled centrally. This creates a powerful environment in which content, technologies and workflows can be precisely coordinated — for professional live communication with maximum control and clear, confident operation.

The entire SLAM.SYSTEMS family was developed with a focus on clear system architecture, intelligent integration and a simple operating philosophy. Operators always work with the familiar SLAM.SYSTEMS interface and system logic — including when adding extensions or moving to future system generations. Connecting individual components creates powerful live environments with a consistent user experience.

Runtime

SLAM PLAYCOR

The intelligent runtime engine for reliable content execution

With SLAM PLAYCOR, the scalable SLAM.SYSTEMS software architecture extends all the way to reliable media output. From individual applications to synchronised stadium and live environments, SLAM PLAYCOR enables precise, accurately timed and reliable playback for professional live applications — with latency below 18 ms.

Each SLAM PLAYCOR can process and output content independently of the overall system. Central assignment and timing control via SLAM DIRECTORCOR bring individual players together in an intelligently coordinated system.

For output scenarios from HD to 8K/UHD, SLAM.SYSTEMS draws on proven hardware concepts and more than 25 years of OOHDT experience. This expertise forms the foundation for reliable solutions, stable operation and clear system architecture that contributes to cost-effective operation over the long term.

For live events with higher availability and operational reliability requirements, SLAM PLAYCOR can operate together with SLAM.SYSTEMS LED displays in a redundant system architecture, where required by the organiser or competition.

SLAM TIMECOR

Live Match Information. Beyond the Field.

SLAM TIMECOR is a complementary runtime module within the SLAM.SYSTEMSCOR Family for displaying current match information beyond the field of play — for example in team and referee changing rooms, VIP areas or control rooms.

During a sporting event, match time and score remain important beyond the field of play. Particularly during breaks, teams and officials need a quick overview of the current score and remaining break time. SLAM PLAYCOR reliably delivers this information to a display.

SLAM TIMECOR is particularly valuable in ice hockey: during breaks, players and staff can view the current score and remaining match time directly in the relevant team and operational areas.
