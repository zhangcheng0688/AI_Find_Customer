B-3.1 / Introduction

SLAM.SYSTEMS LED DISPLAY – LED-VIDEO

Combined with our experience in real applications, this creates LED systems that bring together high image quality, reliable performance, efficient energy use and system architecture designed for the long term.

An LED system is more than a surface for displaying images. It is a professional communication medium that brings content to life, conveys information precisely and creates visual moments that connect with people.

Every application has its own requirements. A stadium, sports hall, conference or control room, or communication space (POI/POS) needs a solution tailored to its image quality, viewing distance, protection rating, brightness and system integration requirements.

The high-end LED systems of the sportselab, sportsevision, rounds and SLAM.SYSTEMS brands are developed and manufactured at Shenzhen sportselab ltd — from technical concepts and mechanical design through production to final quality control.

SLAM.SYSTEMS software solutions and peripherals are developed under Shenzhen Watermoon ltd. This brings in-house software and system development together with professional LED manufacturing in a coordinated technological environment.

For more than ten years, our systems have proven themselves in international elite sport at UEFA, ATP and WTA events, as well as in SFV and SIHF environments. Under demanding conditions, intensive live operation and the scrutiny of modern TV, high-speed and photographic cameras, they demonstrate their image quality, operational dependability and long-term reliability.

One example is our modular LED perimeter system, successfully used in international rental operations for more than ten years and delivering reliable performance at UEFA matches at the highest competitive level.

Our longstanding contacts and proximity to leading higher education institutions in Shenzhen have led to close collaboration with a university. Current scientific and technological findings feed directly into our development, complementing our experience in real applications. This creates LED systems combining high image quality, reliable performance, efficient energy use and architecture designed for the long term.

POIZEN

LED Poster Displays (Foldable, Collective, Cascaded)

POIZEN is a professional LED poster system for attention-grabbing indoor visual communication. New Cold-Screen technology combines high image quality with a robust surface, slim design and efficient operation. With brightness up to 1'200 cd/m², a static contrast ratio of 20'000:1 and 175° viewing angles, content remains clear, colour-stable and visually strong from a wide range of perspectives.

POIZEN can be used as an individual poster on a stand or bracket, cascaded seamlessly into larger display surfaces or integrated into creative installations. The housing is just 44 mm deep and has IP54 protection at the front. Magnetically mounted LED modules and a removable rear cover enable fast front or rear servicing.

For daily operation, POIZEN supports central management and programme updates via LAN, WiFi or 4G. HDMI input and loop output enable cascading of multiple players and multi-window applications. 8 GB of internal storage, USB Plug & Play and WiFi AP control via Android, iOS or PC simplify flexible content delivery in fashion stores, shopping malls, reception areas, airports and showrooms.

The energy-optimised architecture uses around 20% less power than conventional LED posters and reduces panel temperature by approximately 15°C. POIZEN combines strong visual impact with efficient operation, easy maintenance and flexible integration.
