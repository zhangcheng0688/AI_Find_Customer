B-6.1 / Company

SLAM.SYSTEMS

A special moment comes from many elements working together. Information takes shape, content is created, people look for direction, and systems must work together at exactly the right time. What matters is the connection they create.

Ultimately, it is about making the right connection. SLAM.SYSTEMS connects content, systems and people so that communication can make an impact at the decisive moment.

SLAM.SYSTEMS grew from the understanding that impact happens when content, data, systems and people come together. This creates moments that reach people, stir emotions, give meaning and stay in the memory.

The story began in 1999 with the development of multimedia solutions for corporate communication. Concepts such as CORPORATEDIS TV, CORPORATEDIS AUDIO and CORPORATEDIS TXT opened new communication channels, connecting information, content, advertising and entertainment with employees, customers, partners and visitors. This led to the vision of SLAM.SYSTEMS: connecting technology so that content becomes visible, understandable and meaningful at the right moment.

Today's SLAM.SYSTEMS architecture grew from this experience. It connects solutions for sports, businesses and modern communication spaces in one intelligent system. SLAM.SYSTEMS provides the technological foundation: Frontend, Control, Runtime and Output work together as coordinated components.

Sports Moments

Sport is the stage where communication becomes visible at the decisive moment.

SLAM.SYSTEMS connects content, live situations and people, making special moments visible, understandable and tangible.

Connected Spaces

Modern spaces are increasingly becoming places of communication. SLAM.SYSTEMS connects digital content with the people who experience those spaces.

  • Swiss Precision stands for precise engineering, structured system thinking and reliable solutions.
  • Intelligent Innovation comes from understanding future requirements and intelligently connecting modern technologies.
  • Human Connection reminds us that technology creates possibilities — people give them meaning.

2. Scope

This privacy policy explains which personal data we collect and process in connection with our websites, digital platforms, products and services. Personal data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. We process personal data in accordance with the Swiss Data Protection Act. Where the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation applies, we also take its requirements into account.

3. Processing When You Visit Our Websites

When you access our websites, your browser automatically sends technical information to our web server. This may include, in particular:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Page or resource accessed
  • Previously visited page
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Device information
  • Status and error codes

We process this information to provide our websites, ensure technical security, identify errors and prevent misuse.

4. User Accounts and Use of cloud.slam.systems

For registration, licensing and use of cloud.slam.systems, we process the following information in particular:

  • Username
  • Name and company
  • Email address
  • Encrypted or hashed password
  • User and licence permissions
  • Login times and technical log data
  • Contract, order and billing information

We use this information to create and manage user accounts, verify access rights, fulfil contracts and ensure operational and data security. Account data is retained for the duration of the contractual or usage relationship. Business and billing records subject to statutory retention requirements may remain stored for the applicable retention period after an account has been deleted.

5. Contracts, Orders and Payment Processing

To conclude and fulfil contracts and orders, we process the necessary contact, address, contract, order and payment information.

Payment information is only shared with banks and payment service providers needed to process the payment. Business records and contract, address, payment and order data are generally retained for ten years in accordance with statutory retention requirements.

6. Contacting Us

If you contact us through a contact form, by email or through another channel, we process the information you provide to answer your enquiry and communicate with you. We retain this information for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, fulfil a contractual relationship or comply with legal obligations.

7. Newsletter

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we process the following information in particular:

  • Email address
  • Date and time of subscription
  • IP address used to subscribe
  • Record of consent given
  • Technical information about delivery and use, where enabled

The newsletter provides regular or occasional information about our company's products, projects and news. You can unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link in each message or by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that enable technical functions or provide information about website use. Strictly necessary cookies may be used without consent where required for operation, security or the provision of expressly requested functions. Optional cookies for analytics, advertising, personalisation or external media are only used where the necessary consent has been obtained.

Any consent management system used must transparently provide at least the following information:

  • Name of the cookie or service
  • Responsible provider
  • Purpose
  • Storage duration
  • Recipient and recipient country
  • Cookie category
  • Legal basis or justification

You can change your choices or withdraw consent at any time through the cookie settings.

9. Analytics, Advertising and External Content

Where analytics, advertising or external media services are used on our websites, they may process information about your use of our websites. This may include your IP address, device and browser information, content accessed and interactions. Such services, for example Google Analytics, YouTube, Vimeo, LinkedIn or other providers, are activated only in accordance with the applicable data protection provisions and, where required, after you have given consent.

10. Disclosure of Personal Data

We disclose personal data only where necessary to fulfil a contract, operate our services, meet legal obligations or protect legitimate interests, or where appropriate consent has been obtained. Potential recipients include:

  • Hosting and IT service providers
  • Cloud and security service providers
  • Banks and payment service providers
  • Delivery and communication service providers
  • Newsletter and analytics service providers
  • Affiliated companies
  • Authorities, courts or other authorised bodies

When acting as processors, service providers may process personal data only within their contractual duties and according to our instructions. We do not sell personal data or disclose it to address brokers.

11. Processing Data Abroad

Personal data may also be processed outside Switzerland, particularly by affiliated companies or external service providers. If the recipient is in a country whose level of data protection is not recognised as adequate, we provide appropriate safeguards, for example recognised standard contractual clauses, or base the disclosure on a statutory exception.

12. Retention Period

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose. Longer retention may arise in particular from:

  • Statutory retention obligations
  • Ongoing contractual relationships
  • Security and misuse investigations
  • The establishment or defence of legal claims
  • Express consent

Once personal data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised unless a legal or contractual obligation requires further retention.

13. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, unlawful processing or disclosure.

Our websites use an encrypted HTTPS connection. This protects transmission between your browser and our server, but cannot guarantee absolute protection against every risk.

Emails we send are encrypted in transit where the relevant communication service supports the TLS standard. We have no control over the security of an email after delivery or over the recipient's systems.

14. Your Rights

Under the applicable data protection law, you may exercise the following rights in particular:

  • Access to information about the personal data we process
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion of personal data where no retention obligation prevents this
  • Objection to certain processing activities
  • Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future
  • Receipt or transfer of personal data where the legal conditions are met

These rights may be restricted by law. You also have the right to contact the competent data protection supervisory authority.

Please direct enquiries to the controller named above. To protect your data, we may request suitable proof of your identity.

We may update this privacy policy at any time if our data processing, legal requirements or the services we use change. The version published on our websites applies. Last updated: September 2026.
